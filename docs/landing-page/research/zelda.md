## Scope and timebase
Most exact numbers below come from the decompilations (zeldaret/oot, zeldaret/mm, zeldaret/tmc) and the `snesrev/zelda3` C port; "frames" are game ticks: 20 per second in OoT/MM, 60 in ALttP and TMC. Wind Waker has no public decomp I could read, so its entries lean on Zelda Wiki, the cloudmodding format docs, Winditor and Nintendo's HD manual.

## Title screens
- **OoT**: N64 logo, then Link on Epona across Hyrule Field at dawn; logo appears with flames; after Zora River / Kokiri Forest the demo cuts to one of three cutscenes in fixed rotation (Spirit Temple → Death Mountain Crater → Ganondorf on horse) and loops. Logo build-up: flame effect fades in over 40 frames (prim colour (0,100,170) → white) + 40 more to its final tint, shield logo +6 alpha/frame to 210, the "The Legend of / Ocarina of Time" text +2/frame to 255, copyright +6/frame; Start/A/B is accepted 20 frames after the copyright is solid. **"PRESS START" is red (255,30,30) with a 1-px black shadow at x 119, y 174, 7-px letter pitch, 5-px word gap, and it does not blink on/off: alpha = timer×10, timer counts 0→26→0, so a ~52-frame breathing fade.** Pressing a button fades the logo at 25 alpha/frame.
- **MM**: Majora's Mask flies through with motion blur, Mask Salesman laughs, Clock Town day-to-night montage, Skull Kid and Moon, logo fades in (or instantly on any button). "PRESS START" fades 255↔0 in 20-frame steps (40-frame cycle).
- **ALttP (SNES)**: "Nintendo Presents" to the Rupee sound, three Triforce pieces fly in and rotate until they meet, logo, Master Sword strikes down through the Z while the screen flashes; castle-in-lake background; after a wait the Seal of the Seven Maidens story plays and loops. No "press start" copy is described.
- **TMC**: Nintendo + Capcom logos; forest background, Four Sword and logo; USA build then idles 3600 frames (60 s) before looping; the Press Start icon is drawn only when `(timer & 0x20)==0` — a hard 32-on/32-off blink at y=132; A/Start plays SFX_TEXTBOX_SELECT and fades the music.
- **TWW**: the wiki stub has nothing reliable; don't invent.

## File select
- **OoT**: cloudy night sky; three slots show only the name; Copy / Erase / Options below. Labels: "Please select a file", "Open this file?", "Copy which file?", "Copy to which file?", "Are you sure?", "File copied.", "Erase which file?", "File erased.", "File empty/in use". Window panels are blue (100,150,255), inactive grey (100,100,100); names white, inactive grey. **Cursor is a highlight whose alpha pulses 70↔200, 20 frames each way.** Opening a file: other slots fade −25 alpha/frame over 8 frames, the chosen slot slides to the top (16 px per slot) over 8 frames, its info panel fades in +25/frame to 200 showing death count, hearts (255,70,50; defense upgrade = white heart on red 200,0,0), stones/medallions, then Yes/Quit; Yes rumbles (300 strength, 180 frames), plays FSEL_DECIDE_L, screen fades to black +10/frame. Sounds: FSEL_CURSOR, FSEL_DECIDE_S/L, FSEL_CLOSE (back), FSEL_ERROR. Music = Great Fairy theme with its harp intro skipped.
- **MM**: dawn sky; two files (three in Japan); an owl icon beside owl-saved files; expanded info adds rupees, mask count, heart-piece count, boss remains, and for owl saves the day ("First Day/Second Day/Final Day") and clock digits. Highlight colour (155,255,255), 20-frame pulse.
- **ALttP** ("Player Select"): **the cursor is a fairy whose two frames alternate every 8 frames**; each file shows name and hearts; copy/erase steps blink the header stripe with mask 0x30 (hidden 16 of every 64 frames). GBA: Link runs; SNES: static.
- **TWW**: three "Quest Logs" on a waving translucent map sheet over a sky that cycles day/night from the console clock; slots show name + save date/time; after selection they expand to hearts, Triforce Shards, Pearls, and Copy/Delete appear; Second Quest = Triforce icon and yellow panels instead of green. HD manual: "Once you have confirmed a name, it cannot be changed."
- **TMC**: BGM_FILE_SELECT; copy/erase/start; hearts in rows of 10 in quarter units; L + D-pad turns the Link on the file; cursor SFX_TEXTBOX_CHOICE, confirm SFX_TEXTBOX_SELECT, back SFX_MENU_CANCEL, invalid SFX_MENU_ERROR.

## Dialogue box
- **OoT**: 256×64 px box at (34,142) of 320×240 (upper y 38, mid 90); it grows open over 8 frames with an overshoot (width 1.2→2.2→2.0×, height 0.6→1.1→1.0×) while alpha ramps in eighths. Fills: black (0,0,0) α170; wooden (70,50,30) α230; blue (0,10,50) α170; ocarina red (255,0,0) α180. English text at 75 % of 16-px glyphs, 12-px lines, x 65; **one character per frame by default** (0x14 xx adds xx frames per char; 0x08 instant). Colours: white; red (255,60,60); green (70,255,80); blue (80,90,255); light blue (100,180,255); pink (255,150,180); yellow (225,255,50); on a wooden sign red becomes orange (255,120,0). **End icon: a triangle when another box follows, a square on the last box, drawn bottom-centre (box y + 59); it does not blink, its colour crossfades every 12 frames between (0,80,200) and (50,130,255) — blue on N64, green on GameCube.** NA_SE_SY_MESSAGE_PASS on advance, MESSAGE_END when the last box finishes.
- **MM**: same engine; colours red (255,60,60), green (70,255,80), blue (80,90,255), yellow (255,255,50), turquoise (80,150,255), pink (255,150,180), silver (170,170,170), orange (255,130,30); extra box types incl. red boxes and Bombers' Notebook cream (255,255,195).
- **ALttP**: 3-line VWF box with a 3×3 border tile set; letters print at a per-message "Speed"; `Wait` durations 31–500 frames; on A/B the box waits 28 frames then plays sound 36. Commands include Color, Sound, Scroll, Choose.
- **TWW**: text over a translucent black box; draw types "By Char Slow", "By Char Skippable" (A skips), "Instantly"; 4 lines default; "Item Get" style carries an item image. Highlight colours: Light Red #ff5a5a and Vermilion #ff6400 (standard emphasis), Blue #0000aa / Red #b40000 on signs and letters, Green #00ff00 for Tingle, Purple #ff00ff for Rupee amounts, Light Blue #82ffff for the Fairy Queen.
- **TMC**: text speed from the save (ticks per char 5/3/1), holding B ×8; 26×4-tile window; advance cursor tile drawn bottom-right only while waiting; SFX_TEXTBOX_OPEN/CLOSE/NEXT. Colour pairs: Dark Blue/White default, Red/White, Orange/Grey, Gold/White, Light Blue/White, Lime/White.

## Heart meter
OoT/MM: 16-px hearts, first centred at (30,26), 10-px pitch, 10 per row then a second row 10 px down; red (255,70,50) over (50,40,60); defense upgrade draws white (255,255,255) over red (200,0,0) = the white outline; quarter-heart steps. **Only the last heart beats: it shrinks to 68 % over 10 frames and back over 10; at critical health (≤1 heart with ≤5 containers, ≤1.5 up to 10, ≤2 up to 15, else ≤2.75) HITPOINT_ALARM plays once per 20-frame pulse, muted in cutscenes and pause.** ALttP: hearts at tile (20,1), rows of 10, 8 units per heart, half-heart steps; beep (sfx 43) every 32 frames when health ≤1/≤2/≤3 hearts by capacity band. TMC: quarter hearts, rows of 10.

## Item get / "You got …!"
OoT plays NA_SE_SY_GET_ITEM (GET_BOXITEM from chests), Link's `demo_get_itemB` overhead pose, and a black box with the item icon at left (text shifted 32 px). Fanfare: ITEM_GET normally, SMALL_ITEM_GET for heart pieces, HEART_GET for containers / 4th piece; all flagged FANFARE (music pauses). TMC: SFX_ITEM_GET / SFX_HEART_GET; sample copy "You got some Red Picolyte!…". Series: four-note fanfare, five-note for lesser, seven-note for Rupees.

## Fairy fountain and saving
File-select music is the Fairy Fountain theme minus its harp sweeps in nearly every game. OoT fountains: play Zelda's Lullaby on the Triforce mark; revisit heals and refills magic. ALttP Great Fairy heals fully. OoT save: pause → B → prompt (textures SavePrompt, Yes/No, SaveConfirmation, ContinuePlaying); prompt cursor alpha pulses 100↔255; game over offers save-and-respawn, respawn, save-and-title. MM: Song of Time is the full save; owl statues save-and-quit and mark the file. ALttP: Select opens message 0x186; death menu "Save And Continue / Save And Quit / Do Not Save And Continue". TWW: Save on the Items/Quest screens or at game over; Game Over letters drop one by one with thuds; "Continue" or "To Title Screen". TMC: Save button on the pause menu.

## Pause menus
OoT: four pages on a rotating cube (Items, Map, Quest, Equipment), Z/R rotate over 16 frames with WIN_SCROLL_LEFT/RIGHT; pages pitch in from 160°; first pause after boot lands on Map, afterwards Items. MM adds Masks. TWW: green paper Items (21 slots, X/Y/Z assign, Save bottom-left), L/R to Quest Status (songs, shards, pearls, Options: Hold/Switch targeting, rumble, Mono/Stereo/Surround). TMC: Items (12 slots + 4 bottles), Quest Status, Map, L/R switch. ALttP: item window drops from the top.

## Menu sounds
OoT/MM system bank: CURSOR, DECIDE (select/equip), CANCEL, ERROR (unequippable), WIN_OPEN/CLOSE, WIN_SCROLL_LEFT/RIGHT, the five FSEL_ sounds, MESSAGE_PASS/END, MESSAGE_WOMAN/MAN, GET_ITEM, PIECE_OF_HEART, HP_RECOVER, HITPOINT_ALARM, TRE_BOX_APPEAR, CORRECT_CHIME, DEMO_CUT (skip). TMC: TEXTBOX_CHOICE (cursor), TEXTBOX_SELECT (confirm), MENU_CANCEL, MENU_ERROR. ALttP (zelda3 ids): 0x20 cursor, 0x2C confirm, 0x22 erase, 0x3C copy, 36 text advance, 43 low-health beep, 13 heart refill, 18 item menu.

- OoT title demo rotation after the Hyrule Field title: Spirit Temple (Nabooru/Twinrova) → Death Mountain Crater (Sheik) → Ganondorf on horse → back to Spirit Temple (CS_DEST_TITLE_SCREEN_DEMO) (https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/main/src/code/z_demo.c)
- OoT title: N64 logo, Link rides Epona across Hyrule Field at dawn, logo appears with flames, Zora River pause, lens-flare into Kokiri Forest, then one of three cutscenes; MM: Mask flies through with motion blur, Salesman laughs, Clock Town day-to-night montage, Skull Kid and Moon, logo fades in or appears instantly on any button; ALttP: 'Nintendo Presents' with the Rupee sound, Triforce pieces converge, Master Sword strikes through the Z with rapid flashes, Seal of Seven Maidens cutscene loops; TMC: Nintendo and Capcom logos, forest background, Four Sword and logo, 'sits in silence for over a minute' then loops (https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Title_Screen)

- TMC subscreens: pressing START opens one of three Subscreens (Items, Quest Status, Map), L and R switch between them (https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Subscreen)
- TWW Items Screen: green paper-like background, 21 item slots (three rows of four on the left, three columns of three on the right), A shows a description, X/Y/Z assign, Save button in the bottom-left, L/R switches to the Quest Status Screen; TMC Items Screen: 12 fixed item slots plus four bottle slots (https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Inventory)
- TWW Quest Status Screen: green paper background with a large left tab and small right tab, Wind Waker songs with notes, Triforce Shard and Goddess Pearl slots; Options button (targeting Hold/Switch, rumble, Mono/Stereo/Surround) and Save button lower-left; save by pressing START and choosing SAVE on the Items or Quest Status screen (https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Quest_Status_Screen)
- TWW HD manual: a Quest Log slot shows 'New Game' when empty; on selecting a slot you choose Normal Mode or Hero Mode; 'Once you have confirmed a name, it cannot be changed'; data saves on the Items screen or the game-over screen; after saving and quitting you restart on the nearest island or at the start of the dungeon; Copy 'Copy data to a different Quest Log slot, overwriting any data in that slot', Delete 'Select a Quest Log slot to delete its data'; Game Over offers Continue or To Title Screen; Items menu entries Items, Map, Bottles, Save ('Save your progress'), Options (https://csassets.nintendo.com/noaext/image/private/t_KA_PDF/manual-WiiU-The_Legend_of_Zelda_The_Wind_Waker_HD)
- Game Over: OoT menu offers save and respawn, respawn without saving, or save and go to the Title Screen, with bold fiery 'Game Over' text; MM shows no 'Game Over' text; TWW letters fall into place one by one with a thud each, menu: quit, save and quit, or respawn (https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Game_Over)
- ALttP death menu options: 'Save And Continue', 'Save And Quit', 'Do Not Save And Continue' (https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/zelda-a-link-to-the-past/alttp-death-guide)
- MM saving: the Song of Time saves and returns to the First Day; Owl Statues are temporary save-and-quit points (not in the Japanese version) and an Owl mark appears next to the file; in MM3D owl statues save permanently (https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Owl_Statue)
- 'Great Fairy Fountain' (aka 'Fairy Fountain', 'Great Fairy's Fountain') first appeared in ALttP; harp, ethereal upward sweeps then a staccato chromatic melody; 'A version of the theme, with the introductory sweeps skipped, has been used for the File-Selection Screen in nearly every game in the series' (https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Fairy_Fountain_(Theme))
- ALttP's Fairy Fountain is 'used for the start screen and the fountains'; TWW has two versions, one 'carnival-themed'; TMC's File Select version is 'quite a lot bassier' and its fountain version 'slower, warmer, sleepier' (https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/back-page-ranking-every-version-of-fairy-fountain-from-the-legend-of-zelda)
- Great Fairy Fountains: ALttP Great Fairy heals completely on approach; OoT has six, play Zelda's Lullaby on the Triforce mark, revisiting heals wounds and refills magic; MM Great Fairies are shattered into Stray Fairies; TWW fountains are seashell-shaped on the Fairy Islands and give upgrades; TMC has three with quiz-style upgrades (https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Great_Fairy_Fountain)
- Zelda is the Item Get trope codifier: Link holds every new item over his head while a brief fanfare plays; classic four-note fanfare, five notes for lesser items, seven notes for Rupee bundles; later games change Link's expression by item (https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/ItemGet)
- ALttP SNES vs GBA: on the GBA select screen Link runs; on SNES he is static (https://www.zeldadungeon.net/forum/threads/differences-between-snes-and-gba-version.8083/)
- ALttP select screen: the number beside Link counts saves plus deaths (https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/588436-the-legend-of-zelda-a-link-to-the-past/65530677)
- ALttP intro module: Intro_Init plays sound_effect_2 = 10 at the 'Nintendo Presents' screen; Intro_WaitPlayer counts down and then switches main_module_index to 20 (the Attract story module) (https://github.com/snesrev/zelda3/blob/master/src/ending.c)

AVOID
- Do not make OoT's 'PRESS START' a hard on/off blink: it is a slow alpha breath (timer 0→26→0, alpha = timer×10) in red (255,30,30); MM fades 255↔0 over 20 frames each way; only TMC hard-blinks (32 on / 32 off at 60 fps).
- Do not quote OoT/MM frame counts as 60 fps timings: the N64 Zeldas tick 20 times a second, so '12 frames' is 0.6 s, '20 frames' is 1 s.
- Do not use a hand or arrow cursor on an OoT/MM file select: the cursor is a highlight whose alpha pulses 70↔200 over 20-frame legs; ALttP's cursor is a fairy flapping every 8 frames.
- Do not show a triangle on the last text box or a square mid-conversation: OoT/MM use a triangle when more text follows and a square at the end; the icon crossfades colour every 12 frames and does not bob or blink.
- Do not tint the OoT end icon green on an N64-styled box: it is blue (0,80,200 ↔ 50,130,255) on N64 and green only in the GameCube builds.
- Do not use pure #FF0000 for emphasis: OoT red is (255,60,60) and turns orange (255,120,0) on wooden signs; TWW's standard highlight is Light Red #ff5a5a or Vermilion #ff6400, and TMC's is a dark red #b73834.
- Do not beat the whole heart bar: only the current (last) heart pulses, shrinking to 68% over 10 frames and back, and the alarm only sounds at critical health and never in cutscenes or pause.
- Do not give the OoT message box a solid fill or a hard border: it is a 256×64 translucent panel (black α170, wooden α230, blue α170) that grows open over 8 frames with a slight overshoot.
- Do not give Majora's Mask three NTSC save files (it has two; three only in Japan) or forget the owl icon and day/time on owl-saved files.
- Do not treat the file-select music and the fairy fountain music as different pieces: the file select is the Great Fairy theme with its harp intro skipped, in nearly every game.
- Do not write TWW dialogue as black text on white: it sits on a translucent black box, with coloured highlights, and the 'By Char Skippable' typewriter is cut short by A.
- Do not let a 'You got …!' box close itself: in OoT the fanfare and pose hold until the box is dismissed, and the item icon sits at the left with the text pushed 32 px right.
- Do not skip the error sound: invalid actions in OoT/MM play NA_SE_SY_ERROR (FSEL_ERROR on the file select) and in TMC SFX_MENU_ERROR, distinct from the cancel sound.
- Do not invent a Wind Waker title-screen sequence or an ALttP 'Press Start' caption: neither is documented in the sources found.
