## Sources used
The Stardew wiki (Easter Eggs, Secrets, Music, Options, Version History, Modding pages), the modding wiki's Cursors sheet, and the publicly decompiled 1.5.6 game code (WeDias/StardewValley on GitHub: `Menus/TitleMenu.cs`, `LoadGameMenu.cs`, `DialogueBox.cs`, `CharacterCustomization.cs`, `BellsAndWhistles/SpriteText.cs`, `Game1.cs`). Pixel sizes below are at 100 % UI scale; the game's art pixel is 4 screen px (`pixelZoom = 4`), title art is drawn at 3×.

## 1. What is on the title screen
- **Sky**: a flat fill rgb(64,136,248) with a 1-px-wide gradient strip stretched over it, two layers of hills, one big cloud sprite (512×337 at 3×) and 16 small clouds in three sprite variants (123×55, 149×66, 63×37 at 3×), each stacked 36 px higher than the last. Big clouds drift left 0.1 px/frame, small ones 0.3 px/frame, wrapping to the right edge.
- **Birds**: two 26×21 four-frame sprites at 3×, ping-pong at 100 ms/frame, flying left 1 px/frame, starting bottom-right (w−210, h−390) and (w−120, h−360). They exist only in the "sky" phase; the wiki's controller trick (B/O makes clouds rush) "only works where the two birds are flying, and stops upon the menu coming down".
- **Logo**: a 400×187 sprite drawn at 3× (1200×561), centred, ending 100 px from the top (2× on small screens). Leaves and the letters are click targets for the easter eggs (E ×10 → alien; leaves ×10 → three Junimos; W's screw ×10 → butterflies; bottom-left corner → happy face placed in the R → foliage and parrots).
- **Four buttons** New, Load, Co-op, Exit: 74×58 sprites at 3× = 222×174 each, 24 px apart, row centred, bottoms 24 px above the screen edge. They are pictures (wooden signs), not text.
- **Bottom-right**: a "?" About button (22×25 at 3× = 66×75, 48 px from the right, 24 px from the bottom) and, above it, a speech-bubble Language button (27×25 → 81×75). About opens a scrolling credits list; the version ("v1.6.x" + build) is drawn there in SmallFont white at the bottom-left (16 px, 8 px). Language shows 174×78 flag buttons (top half normal, bottom half hover).
- **Corners**: mute-music toggle top-left at (16,16) 36×36; windowed/fullscreen toggle top-right. Both play "drumkit6".
- **Music**: "Stardew Valley Overture" (2:26). Load/Co-op screens switch to "Load Game" (`title_night`, 1:52); New switches to "Cloud Country" (1:30).

## 2. How the title animates in (60 fps timers)
1. **ConcernedApe splash** on white, 5000 ms: fade in over the first 500 ms, out over the last 500 ms; the ape blinks every 100 ms; three "mouseClick" ticks at 1.0 s, 2.5 s, 3.0 s; a launch-count tip in SmallFont, DeepSkyBlue, bottom-left ("Welcome back!" on launch 2, etc.). Clicking the ape: sunglasses off, eyes open, random "Duck" or "fishSlap" sound. Esc/B skips. Main theme starts when it ends.
2. **White fade** (alpha = timer/2000; starts at 4000, so 2 s solid white then 2 s fade), then a **3500 ms pause** on the sky with the birds.
3. **Rise**: the camera value accelerates from 0.05 px/frame by 0.006 px/frame² until it has moved 1000 — about 9.5 s. Parallax: sky gradient and far hills ×0.66, clouds ×0.5, near hills ×1, foreground clouds ×2, logo ×1/3. The logo descends into place from above.
4. **Logo swipe**, 1000 ms: a second copy of the logo pulses up to +0.05 scale about its centre; on finishing, a leaf gust blows off each side (27×21 right, 22×18 left), and later a gust fires at random (0.5 %/frame).
5. **Buttons** appear one by one every 200 ms, each with a "Cowboy_gunshot".

## 3. Hover, click, pointer
- **Hover** on a big button: sprite swaps to its hover row, scale eases up 0.04/frame to 3.25 (from 3), one "Cowboy_Footstep" on entry; About/Language swap sprite and ease to +0.25 the same way. Back (66×27 at 3× = 198×81, bottom-right) does the same.
- **Click** New/Load/Co-op: "select", then the whole scene slides right at 1 px/ms until off-screen while the logo fades (alpha 1 − offset/1200) and, for Load/Co-op, the sky crossfades to the night gradient; a "Loading…" scroll banner sits bottom-left (x 84, y h−64). Back: "bigDeSelect", slide back. Exit: "bigDeSelect", music off, 500 ms fade to black, quit.
- **Pointer**: the game draws its own 16×16 arrow (Cursors sheet 0,0) at 4× (64 px) with a tiny sine pulse; hand (32,0) for pick-up, gift box (48,0), speech balloon (64,0) for talk, magnifier (80,0), hourglass (16,0) added to the title screen in 1.6.9 while loading. Gamepad uses index 44. A "hardware cursor" option exists.

## 4. Load (save-file) screen
A 1100×600 panel (plus border), centred; **four slots per page**, each (width−32)×(height/4), inset 16 px, nine-slice background tinted Wheat on hover; up/down arrows and a scrollbar outside the right edge; wheel and arrows play "shwip". Each slot, left to right: the index "1." in the pixel font; the farmer sprite 16×32 at 4× with a shadow (offset 92,20); the farmer's name in the pixel font (x+164, y+36); the farm name "{name} Farm" right-aligned in dialogueFont (y+44); the save date (day, season, year) in dialogueFont at (x+160, y+104); a coin icon with the money and a clock icon with "H:MM" played, both in the right third; a red X delete button (12×12 at 3×) drawn at 75 % alpha with tooltip "Delete File". **Click**: "select", then 2150 ms: the slot flashes Wheat/White every 75 ms, the last 1000 ms fade the screen to black, then the save loads. A newer-version save plays "cancel" and turns the slot red for 1 s. **Delete**: "drumkit6", 75 % black overlay, a white pixel-font prompt naming the farmer, OK/Cancel, "trashcan" on confirm. **Loading screen** (gameMode 6): the background colour, and "Loading" + 0–3 dots cycling every 333 ms in the pixel font on a scroll banner at bottom-left (x 64, 64 px above the safe-area bottom), its width fixed to "Loading… " so it doesn't jitter.

## 5. Dialogue box and portrait
With a portrait the box is **1200×384**, bottom-centre, 64 px above the bottom. It opens by growing from its centre at 6 px/ms wide (≈200 ms) with "breathin" and closes in reverse with "breathout". Text (pixel font) at (x+8, y+8), 716 px wide; first character after 90 ms, then **one character every 30 ms** (`characterAdvanceDelay = 30`), "dialogueCharacter" per character (Options → "Dialogue Typing Sound", default on) and "dialogueCharacterClose" at the end; a click or right-click reveals the rest, a second click advances after a 750 ms safety delay; a bouncing continue icon shows when typing is done. Portrait: a 115×97 plate at 4× on the right holding the 64×64 portrait at 4× (256 px), the speaker's name centred beneath in the pixel font, and a 44×44 friendship jewel bottom-right; a new emotion shakes the portrait ±1 px for 250 ms. With "Show Portraits" off the text gets a "Name: " prefix. Question boxes list replies under the text; the selected reply gets a box, the others 60 % alpha. Standalone boxes size to the text (width = text + 64, max 1240). There is **no text-speed option**.

## 6. Fonts
- **Pixel font** (`SpriteText`, LooseSprites/font_bold): 8×16 cells indexed by char code − 32, so **digits, UPPER and lower case all exist** (it is not all-caps); drawn at 3× (24×48 px) with a 54 px line pitch; narrow glyphs (i j l ! , .) get −1/−2 kerning; `^` is a newline; 9 colours (black default, white on dark overlays); optional 15 %-alpha shadow. Used for dialogue, names, "Loading…", slot names. Non-Latin languages use a BMFont at 1.5–1.75×.
- **dialogueFont** (Fonts/SpriteFont1) for tooltip titles and slot details; **smallFont** (Fonts/SmallFont, line spacing 26) for tooltip bodies, startup tips, the version; **tinyFont**. The logo is hand-drawn pixel lettering, not a typeface.

## 7. Farm-type selection pattern
1.5: a single column of 88×80 buttons (22×20 icons at 4×) at 88 px pitch inside a 120×652 panel to the right of the character-creation panel, 7 per page with prev/next arrows ("shwip"). Hover eases the icon up 0.1 scale and shows a **tooltip: title in bold dialogueFont over a description in smallFont wrapped at 256 px**, split from a `Name_Description` string. The selected farm gets a highlight nine-slice 4 px taller than the button; locked ones are 50 % black with a lock. 1.6: "two columns instead of one". The in-game descriptions (English wording as surfaced by search, matching the German localisation file): Standard "A simple plot of land with a large amount of open space to design your farm."; Riverland ends "Fish are more common here than usual."; Forest: the forest encroaches on your land; Hill-top: mountainous terrain and a winding river make building hard, but mineral deposits offer mining; Wilderness "There's plenty of good land here, but beware… at night the monsters come out."; Four Corners "The land is divided into four parcels, each with its own perk. Perfect for a group!"; Meadowlands "…a chewy blue grass that animals love. You start with two chickens." Verify exact English in-game before quoting.

AVOID
- Don't set the pixel font in all caps: SpriteText maps char code − 32 into font_bold, so lowercase glyphs exist and 'Loading...' and dialogue are mixed case.
- Don't make the title buttons text: New/Load/Co-op/Exit are 74×58 sign sprites at 3× with a separate hover row, and their labels are part of the art.
- Don't add a text-speed setting as a Stardew convention: the typewriter rate is a fixed 30 ms per character with only a 'Dialogue Typing Sound' toggle.
- Don't put the version number on the title screen itself in 1.6: it is drawn on the About ('?') page, bottom-left in smallFont.
- Don't draw the clouds and hills on one plane: each layer has its own parallax (0.66 / 0.5 / 1 / 2) and the clouds drift continuously (0.1 and 0.3 px/frame).
- Don't fade straight from the splash to the menu: the sequence is white fade, 3.5 s pause on the bird sky, a ~9.5 s accelerating rise, a 1 s logo pulse with leaf gusts, then buttons every 200 ms with a gunshot sound.
- Don't show more than four save slots at once: the Load panel pages by four with up/down arrows and a scrollbar outside the panel.
- Don't load a save instantly: the slot flashes for 1.15 s then the last 1 s fades to black, and the loading screen shows 'Loading' with dots cycling every 333 ms bottom-left.
- Don't use the OS cursor: the game draws its own 16×16 arrow at 4× with a tiny pulse and swaps it (hand, gift, balloon, magnifier, hourglass) by context.
- Don't quote the English farm descriptions from this report without checking in-game: only the German strings and search snippets were verifiable; the 'Name_Description' split and the tooltip layout (bold title + smallFont body wrapped at 256 px) are confirmed from code.
- Don't give the farm picker cards with pictures and body text: the game uses 22×20 icon buttons in a column (two columns in 1.6) and reveals name + description only in the hover tooltip; the selected one gets a highlight box.
