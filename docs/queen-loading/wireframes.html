The queen's loading states: wireframes

She sits in her card. The strip goes under the thread, above the say bar, on her side. Grey boxes stand in for the pack's art; the note under each frame names the piece the finished version uses. Each state borrows a game UI from the early 2000s that we all grew up with.

What makes this possible

Today her runner sends only her words, so the café can't see what she's doing. With this change the runner tells the gateway each step as she takes it (the tool, and the cat it's about), and says "awake" the moment it starts. The page turns that into words with each cat's real name. In claude.ai, where nothing is pushed, the strip still shows "Thinking" and the seconds from your Send until her answer arrives.

With reduced motion the dots, the shimmer, the ▼ and her bob all hold still, and the seconds still count.
