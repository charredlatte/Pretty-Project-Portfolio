## Critical

**1. Messages and files never reach a session, and each try leaves a stray session behind.**
- On 30 September a bound Routine was tested (now in `docs/from-the-litterbox.md`): `create_trigger` with
  `persistent_session_id`, then `fire_trigger`, started a *new* session ("⚡ Catio → name", no repo). The
  target session never saw the text.
- The page still posts this way. `postToSession()` (`bindRoutine()`, then `fire_trigger`) is behind:
  - Send in a cat's conversation;
  - asking the project map a question;
  - every file delivery from the brain;
  - Ask to wrap up.
- When claude.ai lets the call through, the page says "Sent." when nothing arrived, and a stray session
  appears in her list.
- The test suite checks the broken behaviour as correct: "sending keeps the file, binds a Routine to the
  session once, and fires it with the delivery". The stub can't know that the real tools behave differently.
- **Fix:**
  1. Stop binding Routines now: every post goes to `outbox/` with `why: "no_route"`, and the toast says it is
     waiting.
  2. Then try Claude Code Remote's `send_message`. Its own tool descriptions refer to it ("steering it with
     send_message"). Declare it, check it once with her OK on a test session, and switch to it if it posts
     into the session.
  3. Change the test to expect what the page should do, not what the stub allows.

## Warning

**2. The "need you" count is inflated by cats that will never stop needing her.**
- Only sleeping cats go up to the attic (`STALE_DAYS`). Review-ready cats stay "to review" for ever, and
  month-old Remote Control sessions keep the brand's badge high (`docs/from-the-litterbox.md`).
- The warm-start placeholder session (`__warming__`, tag `cowork-warm-start`) shows up as a cat to review.
- **Fix:**
  - send review-ready cats older than `STALE_DAYS` to the attic too;
  - skip sessions tagged `cowork-warm-start`.

  The brand's badge must be something she can trust, the same rule CLAUDE.md sets for the queens.

**3. Most of the UI audit of 29 September is still open** (now in `docs/from-the-litterbox.md`, under Findings). Fixed since:
- the hover-steal (menus now open on a click);
- a queen's notes saving as they change;
- `aria-expanded` on the House button and the fold;
- 44 px touch targets on the panel.

Still open:
- errors and "queued" messages vanish after 3.2 s (`toast()`), with no `role="alert"`;
- the brain's sorter has no time limit, so a hung sorter keeps Send disabled for ever, and files over 20 MB
  are skipped without a word;
- speech bubbles are still Tab stops, which breaks "the map is one Tab stop";
- there is no way to pause the cats' motion on the page (WCAG 2.2.2), only the system's reduced-motion
  setting;
- the input placeholders are 3.3:1 contrast (`#5F6A5E` on `#C1C8B9`);
- Nunito text at 10 to 12.8 px in bubbles, plaques and counts.

**4. The plugin that lets someone else run a Catio describes a house that no longer exists.**
`catio-plugin/skills/catio/SKILL.md` still talks about:
- the cabin and `cabin.py` (deleted);
- six zips (it's ten);
- Cosy Cabin furniture "already committed" as the whole house.

Anyone following it today fails at the build step. The README's "Five of the six packs" is stale for the same
reason.

**5. `main` is behind the live page.** `main` has version 12 (PR #6). Versions 13 to 16 live only on
branches. My branch has all of them merged and tested.
- **Fix:** open a pull request from `claude/dreamy-goldberg-iu6w6l` to `main`, then retire the 14 old
  branches.
- The danger is real: any old branch can still publish over the live page. That happened twice on 30
  September.

**6. The Pastel credit is hidden on phones.** `.credits { display: none }` under 560 px. SC_siosio's
licence says the credit "must not be intentionally hidden".
- **Fix:** put the credits line at the foot of the House menu, where a phone can reach it.

## Suggestion

**7. CLAUDE.md contradicts itself in two places.**
- Its publish section says "the declaration is `{ mcp: list_sessions }, db`", while its harness section gives
  the full set (nine Claude Code Remote tools, `host:catio`, `assets`, `sample`).
- Its harness section ends with a "Still to do" line that was done in versions 8 to 12.

Both are fixed with this audit. It also says "omit `capabilities` to keep what's stored", which is what has
been done. `host:catio` can only be declared from the desktop app, so the "full set" can't be published from
here anyway.

**8. `docs/renovation-mode.md` rests on decisions that have since changed.** It says:
- the room signs and badges stay (version 13 removed them);
- menus open on hover (they open on a click);
- the defaults come from `cabin.py` (gone: `MANOR.layout`, and the furniture is already sprites);
- the filing-cabinet question is open (decided 30 September).

It is rewritten with this audit as the renovation mode spec.

**9. The plan is a diary.** `docs/plan.md` had grown to a log of every version, with "Done" sections citing
97 and 115 passing checks. The log moves to `docs/history.md`, and the plan becomes what's next, in order.

**10. Small loose ends that cost nothing to close:**
- `harness/skills/graphify/LICENSE-MIT` is missing, though its `NOTICE` refers to it;
- `save-sessions.py` drops `environment_id` and the model, so the saved copy shows no breeds;
- `bundle.py` leaves `catio_mcp.py` and `rules.json` out of the localhost folder;
- the litter box isn't on the wishlist yet.
