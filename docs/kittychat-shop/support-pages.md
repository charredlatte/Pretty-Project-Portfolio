campaign (her pick, 4 October, settling "Ulule or Kickstarter" and "Buy Me a Coffee or Ko-fi"). Both accounts are

## Before either page goes up: the art

**Neither page may show the café as it looks today.** Taking money for the café is commercial use, and most of
what's on screen is from packs that forbid it (`CLAUDE.md`, "Licences"; `docs/from-the-litterbox.md`, "If the Catio
ever becomes commercial"):

|---|---|---|
| Menus, buttons, pixel font, mood faces, paw pointer | Sprout Lands UI, basic | **No**: non-commercial |
| The cats, the logo | ToffeeCraft, free | **No**: personal use only |
| Flowers on the lawn | Sprout Lands Sprites, basic | **No**: non-commercial |
| Glazed tiles, the forest | Little Dreamyland, free | **No**: non-commercial |
| Garden walls and hedges | Top Down Garden Castle | **No**: no distribution at all |
| Stonework | Cainos, Pixel Art Top Down Basic | Free for any project, but no redistribution: keep it small in a picture, never a download |
| Furniture | Cosy Cabin | Yes, with credit to Marie Pepo |
| The map panel, the corner icons | Game UI Pack Pastel | Yes, credited word for word: "Game UI Pack created by SC_siosio" |
| Her own drawings (`docs/drawing-plan.md`) | hers | Yes |

Three ways through, cheapest first:

1. **Words and her own drawings only.** The coffee page needs no screenshot: a profile picture she drew and the
   text below are enough. This is how to open the coffee page now.
2. **Buy the commercial versions** of what's on screen: the premium Sprout Lands packs and the paid ToffeeCraft
   packs allow commercial use (`docs/from-the-litterbox.md`). Little Dreamyland and Heosphorus's pieces still have
   to come out of the picture, or be swapped for her own.
3. **Her own art throughout** (`docs/drawing-plan.md`), which the hosted café and the app need anyway. The Ulule
   campaign's video and pictures wait for 2 or 3.

## Buy Me a Coffee

**The handle `kittychat` is free** (checked 4 October: buymeacoffee.com/kittychat answers 404, a taken handle
answers 200). `kittychatcafe` is free too, as a second choice.

### Signing up, step by step

1. buymeacoffee.com → **Start my page** → sign up with her email or Google.
2. Page address: `kittychat`.
3. Name: `KittyChat Café`. Profile picture: one of her own drawings, never a pack's cat or the ToffeeCraft logo.
4. **Payouts**: connect Stripe (France is supported). Stripe asks for her legal name, date of birth, address, an
   IBAN and an ID photo; as an *entreprise individuelle* she gives the SIREN too. Use the same identity as the shop's
   mentions légales.
5. Currency: **EUR**. Price of one coffee: **3 €** (the page's buttons then offer 1, 3 or 5 coffees).
6. Paste the "About" text, switch on **Memberships**, add the tiers and the thank-you message below.
7. Leave **Shop** and **Commissions** off: the paid plans are sold on the KittyChat Shopify store, with its CGV and
   the waiver of the 14-day withdrawal right, which Buy Me a Coffee's checkout doesn't carry.

What it costs her: 5 % to Buy Me a Coffee on each payment, plus Stripe's own fee. No monthly charge.

## Ulule

**Open the account now, launch later.** The README promises the campaign "once the first cafés are open", and its
rewards (a year of the hosted café, a drawn cat) can't be delivered until sign-up exists (phase 2 of
`docs/accounts.md`) and the art is commercial-safe (above). A draft costs nothing and Ulule's team reviews it before
it goes live, so the copy can be ready long before.

### Signing up, step by step

1. ulule.com → **Créer un projet** → sign up with her email.
2. Type: **Projet avec contreparties** (rewards). Category: *Jeux* or *Technologie*.
3. Funding: **tout ou rien** (all or nothing, Ulule's default): nothing is collected unless the goal is reached,
   so a missed goal owes no rewards.
4. Project page address: `kittychat-cafe` (free on 4 October).
5. Paste the copy and the rewards below. Goal: `[goal]` from the business plan.
6. Before launch, Ulule asks for her identity, an IBAN and, for rewards that are sales, her legal status: the same
   EI and SIREN as the shop. Each reward is a sale: on the shop's VAT footing ("TVA non applicable, art. 293 B du
   CGI"), counted in her turnover.
7. Submit for review only when the pictures are commercial-safe and sign-up exists.

What it costs her: Ulule's commission on what's collected (backers pay a separate service fee since April 2025).
The business plan has the rate.
